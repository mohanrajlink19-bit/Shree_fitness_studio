# Google Business Profile & local search playbook

This is how Shree Fitness Unisex Studio AC can show up when someone nearby searches **"gym near me"**, **"gym in Porur"**, **"personal trainer near me"** or **"AC gym Porur"**.

## How Google picks the gyms it shows

"Near me" results come mostly from the **Google Maps / local 3-pack**, not the normal blue links. Google ranks that list on three things:

| Factor | What it means for Shree Fitness | Can we change it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Distance** | How close the searcher is to the studio pin | No. You will rank best for people within roughly 1–3 km of Porur |
| **Relevance** | How well the profile and website match the search (categories, services, description, website text, review wording) | Yes. This guide covers it |
| **Prominence** | How well-known and trusted the business is: number of reviews, how recent they are, the rating, owner replies, links and directory listings, photos and activity | Yes. **This is the biggest lever.** Reviews matter most |

Nobody can guarantee the #1 spot. Don't trust anyone who sells that. A realistic goal is to be in the **top 3 for people searching from Porur and the next localities**, and #1 for "Shree Fitness" and "unisex AC gym Porur".

## 3. Reviews: the #1 lever for "gym near me"

Review **count, rating, recency and owner replies** drive both prominence and clicks. Gyms in Porur compete heavily here. Check the current top 3 for "gym in Porur" on Google Maps, note their review counts, and set a target to pass them.

**Set up the engine**

1. In the profile, use **"Ask for reviews"** to copy the short review link.
2. Turn the link into a QR code (any free QR generator). Print it at the front desk and on the mirror near the exit: *"Enjoying your training? Tell Porur about it ⭐"*
3. Ask in person and then on WhatsApp, about **2–3 weeks after someone joins** (when they are happiest) and after milestones.
4. Aim for a **steady flow every week**, not a burst. A sudden spike looks fake and can get reviews filtered.

**WhatsApp request template**

> Hi {name}, thank you for training with us at Shree Fitness! 💪 If you have a minute, an honest Google review really helps people in Porur find the gym: {review link}. Mentioning what you liked (equipment, trainer, timings, AC) helps others too. — Sakthi Master

**Reply to every review within 48 hours.** Use the reviewer's name, something specific, and a natural mention of the service:

> *Positive:* "Thank you, {name}! Great to see your consistency on the morning batch. See you on the floor! – Sakthi Master, Shree Fitness Porur"
>
> *Negative:* "Sorry to hear this, {name}. We'd like to fix it. Please call us on 95660 99329 so we can talk. – Shree Fitness"

**Never:** buy reviews, give discounts or free sessions in exchange for reviews, write reviews for members, ask only happy members ("review gating"), or post reviews from staff or family. Google removes these, and the listing can be penalised.

## 8. Search Console, Bing and measuring results

1. **Google Search Console** → Add property → **URL prefix** → `https://shreefitnessstudio.pages.dev/` → verify with the **HTML tag** method. Paste the `<meta name="google-site-verification" …>` tag into the `<head>` of `index.html`, rebuild and redeploy. Domain (DNS) verification is not possible on a `pages.dev` address.
2. Submit `sitemap.xml`. Use **URL Inspection → Request indexing** for `/`, `/personal-training`, `/online-training` and `/booking`.
3. **Bing Webmaster Tools** → import from Search Console (one click).
4. Add **Cloudflare Web Analytics** (free, no cookies) so the GBP UTM links show up as visits.
5. Every month, record from the GBP **Performance** tab: searches, calls, direction requests, website clicks and bookings. Also record Search Console clicks for "gym in Porur", "gym near me" and "personal trainer".
6. Spot-check rankings on a phone (location on, incognito) for "gym near me" from four places: Porur Junction, Ramapuram, Valasaravakkam and Iyyappanthangal. A free grid tool such as *GMB Everywhere* or *Local Falcon*'s trial shows this as a map.
