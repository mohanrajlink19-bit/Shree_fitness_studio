ONLINE TRAINING QUESTIONS

GOOD
TO know.

Do I need gym equipment to train online?+

No. Tell the trainer what you have — no equipment, a mat and light equipment, dumbbells or resistance bands, or access to a gym — and sessions are planned around it.

Which devices can I use?+

You can join live workout sessions from a mobile, tablet or laptop.

How much does online training cost?+

Session timings, equipment needs and fees are agreed with the trainer. Book an online consultation or call 8778642342 to discuss current fees.

Is the diet-plan guidance a medical nutrition plan?+

No. Diet-plan guidance covers everyday food habits that support your training. If you have a medical condition, speak with your doctor about your diet.

Can I join from outside Chennai?+

Online sessions are held live over the internet, so you do not need to be near the studio. Ask about session times (Chennai time, IST) when you book, and include your country code if you are outside India.
